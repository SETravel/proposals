Curated For Long-Time Visitors

A Newer Cayman

◆ ◆ ◆
You already know Stingray City, Rum Point, Hell, and the Turtle Centre — so this section skips all of that. Tap any that interest you and I'll book or research further. These are restaurants and experiences that have opened or come into their own since you've been making this trip — the kind of things even locals are still discovering.
Newer Experiences & Excursions
Cayman Crystal Caves — Northside
Top 5 Attractions in the World (Tripadvisor 2024). Three crystal-formation caves you can walk through with a guide. ~$50/pp direct · ~$143/pp with hotel transport
Bioluminescent Bay Tour — Bio Bay, NE Coast
Evening kayak or boat tour through glowing dinoflagellate waters. Moonless nights are best. ~$120–$160/pp
Pampered Ponies Beach Ride — Barker's National Park, West Bay
Ride horses through shallow Caribbean water on a secluded beach. Suitable for all levels. ~$130/pp
Camana Bay Wednesday Farmers & Artisans Market
Local farmers, artisans, jewelry, and live demos along the Paseo. Wed 6/17 · 10am–4pm · Free
Davinoff's Sculpture Garden (now at Botanic Park) — East End
Oversized concrete iguanas and sea turtles that moved to the Botanic Park's new children's area. Combine with Blue Iguana habitat. ~$15/pp park entry
Starfish Point — North Side
A quieter, shallower alternative to the Sandbar — wild starfish you can stand among, no boat required. Free · driveable
Newer Restaurants on Seven Mile Beach & Camana Bay
Uncle Liu's — Seven Mile Beach
Opened 2025. Authentic Chinese with Caribbean twists — try the steamed snapper or coconut lobster. ~$30–55/pp
Kojima Izakaya — George Town Waterfront
Newer Japanese izakaya with panoramic ocean views — sushi, yakitori, fresh sashimi. ~$50–85/pp
Allora Cayman — Camana Bay
Wood-fired Italian pizzeria & bakery — handcrafted ingredients, fresh pastries daily. Quick rave-review climb. ~$25–45/pp
LOCO — Camana Bay
Camana Bay's newest Mexican spot with a serious tequila & mezcal bar. Tableside guacamole, modern setting. ~$35–60/pp
Cask — Camana Bay
Cayman's first dedicated whisky bar — global selection, curated food pairings. Great spot for Billy. ~$25–60 cocktails & small plates
Ascia — at the Grand Cayman Marriott
Marriott's newest signature restaurant — Asian fusion with Nikkei (Japanese-Peruvian) flair. ~$60–95/pp
The Brooklyn — Camana Bay
Italian-American family-friendly classics with a twist. Great group dinner spot. ~$30–55/pp
Union Grill & Bar — Camana Bay (waterfront)
'Best Restaurant in the Cayman Islands' winner. Live jazz Thursdays, soul/blues Sat-Sun. Dry-aged steaks, fresh fish. ~$60–110/pp
