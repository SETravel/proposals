The Lower Mississippi route is steeped in the history and culture that shaped America — from the storied streets of Memphis and the echoes of the Civil War at Vicksburg, to the antebellum grandeur of Natchez and the legendary energy of New Orleans. ACL's all-inclusive format means the Pattersons never reach for their wallets once onboard.

Agent's Note: July on the Lower Mississippi runs warm and humid — expect highs in the 90s. The ship's air-conditioning is excellent, and most excursions are morning-focused to beat the heat. If the Pattersons are heat-sensitive, the Upper Mississippi (Option 2) offers significantly more comfortable July weather. Also worth noting: the Graceland pre-cruise package (The Guest House at Graceland) is a spectacular add-on for any Elvis fans — confirm availability when booking.

The Upper Mississippi is the Pattersons' most scenic option in July — towering bluffs, wildlife-rich shorelines, and the kind of unhurried Midwestern river towns that feel straight out of a Twain novel. Hannibal, Missouri alone is worth the trip. This route also benefits from ACL's current Free Airfare promotion, which could save the Pattersons a meaningful amount depending on their departure gateway.

Agent's Note: This is my top recommendation for a July 2026 departure. The Upper Mississippi offers the most comfortable summer weather of the three options, and the Free Airfare promotion makes the value exceptional. The four-story glass atrium on the American Serenade is spectacular on this route — watching the bluffs pass from the atrium lounge is a one-of-a-kind experience. Confirm the airfare promotion applies to the July 7 departure when booking — it's capacity-controlled.

The Viking Mississippi stands apart from every other ship on the river. Purpose-built and launched in 2022, it brings Viking's world-class design philosophy to American waters — the same Scandinavian elegance found on their legendary European longships, reimagined for the Mississippi. The Explorers' Lounge spans two stories of panoramic river views, and the Aquavit Terrace offers al fresco dining you won't find anywhere else on the river.

Agent's Note: Viking pricing shown above is an estimate for July 2026 — exact rates vary by departure date and must be confirmed directly. Viking includes daily excursions, meals, and beer/wine at meals, but spirits and specialty tours carry additional costs (unlike ACL's fully all-inclusive model). Also note: at 386 guests, this is a notably larger ship than ACL's 150–175 passenger vessels. For clients who prefer a more intimate, small-ship feel, ACL is the stronger choice. For those who want modern amenities and a larger ship, Viking delivers.

Shore Experiences
Explore More at Every Port
Every cruise includes complimentary featured excursions at each port — but here are some premium experiences worth adding to make each stop unforgettable. Tap any that interest you and we'll make sure they're on your radar when we book.
MEMPHIS, TN — LOWER MISSISSIPPI
VIP Graceland Tour — The Guest House at Graceland
2-night pre-cruise package with hotel, breakfast, private Graceland archive access, and VIP reception. A bucket-list Elvis experience. · ~$55/pp for tour; hotel package pricing varies
Sun Studio Tour — Birthplace of Rock 'n' Roll
Walk the floors where Elvis, Johnny Cash, and B.B. King recorded. One of America's most hallowed musical sites. · ~$15/pp
Beale Street Evening — Blues & BBQ
Memphis's legendary music strip at night — live blues, world-famous BBQ joints, and a genuine Southern atmosphere. · ~$30–60/pp for dinner + covers
NEW ORLEANS, LA — LOWER MISSISSIPPI
National WWII Museum
One of the finest museums in the country — deeply moving, brilliantly curated. Plan at least half a day. · ~$30/pp
Preservation Hall Jazz Concert
Iconic New Orleans jazz in its most authentic form — intimate hall, living legends, unforgettable atmosphere. · ~$35/pp
HANNIBAL, MO — UPPER MISSISSIPPI
Mark Twain Boyhood Home & Museum
Walk through the rooms that shaped Tom Sawyer and Huckleberry Finn, guided by a local storyteller. · ~$15/pp
Mark Twain Cave — Underground Adventure
One of the region's most famous natural landmarks, referenced in Twain's own writings. Narrated cave exploration. · ~$25/pp
ST. PAUL, MN — UPPER MISSISSIPPI
Science Museum of Minnesota
A world-class natural history and science museum right on the Mississippi riverfront. Perfect for curious minds. · ~$18/pp
Summit Avenue Walking Tour
F. Scott Fitzgerald's neighborhood — Victorian mansions, historic churches, and stunning river views from the bluff. · Free – $15/pp with guide
