Agent's Note
The $172.80 per-person fare is United's Basic Economy. Couple things to know about this fare class: it does not include a full-size carry-on bag (personal item only — small bag that fits under the seat), checked bags run $50 each way per person, and seats are assigned at check-in so you may not be seated together on the plane. For a three-week trip we typically recommend upgrading to Main Cabin (United Economy), which adds roughly $60–$100 per person each way but allows carry-ons, advance seat selection, and standard baggage rules. Just say the word and we can re-price at the Main Cabin tier before you book. Return flight also has an earlier 8:25 AM departure option if you prefer arriving home earlier in the day instead of just before midnight.

SFO has the largest rental car center in Northern California with every major supplier on-site. After landing, the AirTrain from the terminal to the rental center takes about 8 minutes, then you are in your car and on your way. Return is just as easy — drop the car, AirTrain to your departure terminal. Three full weeks of unrestricted Northern California exploration: city, coast, wine country, redwoods, whatever the itinerary calls for.
Agent's Note
Late July is peak rental season at SFO so the published rates run higher than other times of year — current daily rates for a standard sedan run around $55/day base. California airport rentals carry roughly 22–25% in concession fees, customer facility charges, and state tax on top, so the realistic all-in for 21 days lands around $1,400–$1,450. The exact final number depends on which supplier we book — we will run the rate through our agency channels before we confirm to get you the best available price, which is typically better than what shows up on the public booking sites. One Bay Area note: rental car break-ins at airport lots and tourist parking are common — never leave anything visible in the car when parked.
