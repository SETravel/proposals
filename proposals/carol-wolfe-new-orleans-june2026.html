ADDITIONAL OPTIONS — WE'D BE HAPPY TO BOOK ANY OF THESE
Commander's Palace — Lunch or Dinner
New Orleans' most celebrated dining institution — a Garden District landmark with impeccable Creole cuisine, white-tablecloth elegance, and the famous 25¢ martinis at lunch. A short ride from the French Quarter.
~$65–85/person · We'll handle the reservation
Steamboat Natchez — Jazz Dinner Cruise
Board an authentic steam-powered paddlewheeler right on the Mississippi for a 2-hour evening cruise with live jazz, dinner, and spectacular city views. One of the most relaxing and quintessentially New Orleans experiences there is.
~$50–75/person depending on dinner package · Departs from the French Quarter
Arnaud's — Classic French Quarter Dinner
A French Quarter institution since 1918 — all dark wood, mosaic tile floors, and ceiling fans. Known for shrimp Arnaud, bananas foster, and a timeless Creole atmosphere. The attached Remoulade offers a more casual (and affordable) option next door.
~$55–75/person at Arnaud's · ~$20–35/person at Remoulade next door
