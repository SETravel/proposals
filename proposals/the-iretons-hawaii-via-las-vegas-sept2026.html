Snorkel from the Sand

Beach-Entry Snorkeling

◆
◆
◆
All shore-entry, no boats. You can wade in and start snorkeling without ever stepping onto a catamaran. Four options below — Lynne's guided class (bundled into your Moana package, no extra cost), the Hanauma Bay day-trip, and two free Waikiki beaches you can roll up to anytime.
~30 min east of Waikiki
Hanauma Bay Nature Preserve

A protected volcanic crater bay, voted Best Beach in America, with 400+ fish species and Hawaiian green sea turtles in shallow, calm water. The single best beach snorkel on Oahu — and the one you genuinely shouldn't miss.

Entry
$25/pp · ages 13+

From Waikiki
Roberts shuttle
How we'll book it
The standard reservation system only opens two days in advance and sells out in minutes — which is why most visitors who try to DIY this never get in. We'll book the Roberts Hawaii round-trip shuttle from Waikiki (~$30–45/pp), which includes guaranteed admission, bypasses the reservation lottery, and solves the parking problem. Wednesday Sept 23 is the call — the bay reopens after the Mon–Tue closure with the clearest water of the week. Thursday Sept 24 is the backup. One practical note: the path from the rim down to the beach is steep, but there's a shuttle tram that runs both ways — we'll plan around it.
Walking distance · 1 hour · Tue Sept 22
Lynne's Guided Waikiki Class

A small-group guided snorkel session in the Waikiki Marine Conservation Area, led by Lynne — 10.0/10 on Viator across 37 reviews (every single review is a 10). Equipment, free photos, and a compilation video included. Pitched by the operator as the in-Waikiki alternative to Hanauma Bay, in shallow protected water (max 5 ft) with abundant fish. The perfect first-snorkel of the trip.

Why Tuesday morning works
Tuesday Sept 22 lands perfectly between your Saturday arrival and the Hanauma Bay day on Wednesday — and Tuesdays are the days Hanauma is closed anyway, so this is the natural 'first snorkel' of the week. Lynne provides everything (snorkel, mask, water shoes in sizes 3–13, floaty noodle), takes photos and a compilation video for you, and is walking distance from the Moana. This class is bundled into both Moana packages above — no separate booking needed. Free cancellation through Mon Sept 21. One note from the operator: the entry has thick sand with a rocky bottom, so leg stability matters — if you'd rather skip a class with that kind of entry, Kuhio Beach below is the calmer free alternative.
0 min · steps from the Moana
Kuhio Beach

The protected swim basin in central Waikiki, walled off by a sea wall that creates a calm, shallow lagoon. Quietest snorkeling close to the hotel — a few tropical fish in the rocks, easy entry, and the city's free beach-wheelchair program runs right out of this stretch of sand.

Good for the easy days
Kuhio is the right call for the in-between days — when Hanauma Bay is closed (Mon/Tue), when the weather is unpredictable, or when you just want to be in the water without going anywhere. The beach wheelchair lives on this stretch and we'll have it pre-reserved through the City & County program. Not as much marine life as Hanauma Bay, but the convenience is unmatched.
~10 min · east end of Waikiki
Sans Souci Beach (Kaimana)

The quieter, locals' end of Waikiki, tucked between Kapiolani Park and the New Otani — calmer water, a small reef channel with reliable fish, and a fraction of the foot traffic of central Waikiki. A short ride from the Moana.

The hidden gem
Sans Souci has the best beach snorkel actually in Waikiki — the small reef on the east side of the swim area regularly hosts honu (green sea turtles) and surgeonfish. The trade-off is the transfer (it's not within rolling distance of the Moana on the sand — we'd grab a van for the 5–8 min ride). Pair it with a lunch at the Hau Tree right behind the beach.
