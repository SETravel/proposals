Agent Notes: Late June is summer peak at LAX — daily rates run higher than spring/fall. Your best bet is booking 2–4 weeks out through Alamo (top-rated on-airport provider), Hertz, or National. All LAX rental companies operate from the off-terminal CONRAC — free shuttle, about 10 minutes from any terminal. Confirm pick-up hours in advance. Taxes, airport fees, and the California CFC add roughly $50–$80 on top of the base rate for a 5-day rental — always compare the final total, not the daily headline rate.
