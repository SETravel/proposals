The YQ Guarantee gets you into an Ocean View stateroom at the lowest possible price point — with one significant catch: you surrender all say in your room location. The cruise line will assign your deck and cabin, which could land you near the bow or stern on a lower deck, potentially above entertainment venues. Agent's Note

This is the only OV option with confirmed all-in pricing — taxes and port fees of approximately $130.94 per person are included in the $2,657.76 total. All other Ocean View options below carry an NRD (Non-Refundable Deposit) rate notation, and it should be confirmed whether taxes are additional on those. The YQ saves you money but gives up location control entirely. If cabin placement matters — mid-ship, away from noisy venues, higher deck for less motion — step up to the 1N at minimum.

Standard Ocean View
These two categories are functionally identical in size and layout — same square footage, same window, same amenities. The only difference is location on the ship. The 3N sits on lower decks in less premium spots; the 1N is placed in a more desirable position (mid-ship or higher decks). You'll pay $40 more for the 1N — and you choose your exact room number with both. Tap either price bar to select.
