Next: Airport Transfers
Ground Transport
SJD Airport ↔ Villa del Palmar
Villa del Palmar is 28 miles from SJD — a 30 to 40 minute drive along the Transpeninsular Highway. One thing worth knowing upfront: Uber is not reliably available for airport pickups at SJD. Local taxi associations restrict ride-share access at the terminal, so pre-booking a transfer is the way to go.
🏨
Resort Transfer
Pricing on request
Villa del Palmar offers their own round-trip airport transfer service. Book through the resort directly — the smoothest option for a stress-free start. Their team can arrange to match your flight arrival.
🚐
Private Shuttle · SJD Taxi
~$100 one-way · ~$190 round-trip
Private SUV for up to 4 passengers. Driver meets you at arrivals with a sign, handles all luggage, and includes cold water and beverages. Flight tracking means they know when you land.
🚕
Airport Taxi Kiosk
~$80–$100 one-way (up to 4)
Regulated taxi kiosks inside the SJD terminal. Flat zone-based fare paid before reaching the vehicle. No pre-booking required — convenient but no meet-and-greet or luggage help.
🚌
Shared Shuttle
~$20–$40 per person one-way
Most economical option. English-speaking drivers and air-conditioned vehicles. Makes multiple stops along the hotel corridor — adds 15–30 minutes but a solid value for two.
About Uber in Los Cabos

Uber is available within Cabo San Lucas for getting around town once you're settled — and it works well for restaurants, marina trips, and excursions. However, Uber drivers are not permitted to pick up at SJD airport (local taxi unions actively enforce this). For airport arrivals and your July 7 departure, pre-book one of the options above. We can arrange any of these on your behalf.
